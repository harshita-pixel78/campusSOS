SMART CAMPUS PROBLEM REPORTING

CampusSOS gives students one simple platform to report campus problems, automatically prioritize them, route them to the right department and track their resolution.

HACKATHON MVP • CAMPUSSOS

Campus problems shouldn't get lost.

Broken facilities, Wi-Fi failures, water leaks, cleanliness issues and safety concerns are often reported through scattered channels.

Students may not know who to contact, while authorities have to manually sort and prioritize complaints.

CampusSOS creates one centralized workflow for reporting, triaging, routing and tracking campus issues.

01

Easy Reporting

Students can submit a campus problem with a short description and location.

02

Smart Triage

The system automatically identifies the category, priority and responsible department.

03

Complaint Tracking

Every complaint receives a unique ID that students can use to monitor progress.

04

Admin Dashboard

Authorities can search complaints, filter them and update their status.
