The Problem

Campus problems shouldn't disappear into silence.

Students often report problems through scattered channels, while administrators have limited visibility into what needs attention first.

01

Scattered reporting

Complaints can get buried in messages, conversations, or informal requests.

02

No clear priority

A leaking tap and an electrical hazard shouldn't receive the same level of urgency.

03

No visibility

Students often don't know whether their complaint was received, assigned, or resolved.

04

No feedback loop

Students should be able to confirm whether a reported issue was actually fixed.

CampusSOS

One simple workflow.

Designed around three principles: easy, fast, and effective.

01

Report quickly

Describe the problem and location without unnecessary forms.

02

Smart Triage

The system identifies category, priority, department, and urgency from the description.

03

Track progress

Students can follow the complaint from submission to resolution.

04

Close the loop

Students can confirm a resolution or reopen an issue if it wasn't actually fixed.
